How to read the views

Geospatial uses supplied bus coordinates and line geometry. Buses without coordinates stay explicitly unplaced.

Single-wire collapses phase conductors into one engineering-style overview: heavy lines are busbars, paired coils are transformers, and open blades are switches.

Multi-wire expands terminal maps for a selected bus or device. Phase, neutral, and ground cues are labelled as well as patterned.

Results colour and pattern loading, voltage deviation, and operating state. Hover a mark for a compact metric summary; select it for the full record.

Keyboard: Tab to an asset, Enter or Space to select, and use the inventory search arrows plus Enter to jump to a ranked match. In Single-wire, choose the direction/root, then select a bus to nudge and lock its position; layout adjustments are saved locally per case.
